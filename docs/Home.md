# Until — Documentation

Until is a personal productivity system built around **horizons** — projects and commitments organized by temporal proximity rather than as a grid of calendar slots. This is the documentation wiki; the [repo README](../README.md) is just the front door.

## Concepts at a glance

- **Horizons, not calendars.** Projects grouped by how soon they matter: *Short Horizon* (imminent), *Horizon* (weeks–months), *Long Horizon* (6 months+), *Over The Horizon* (overdue).
- **Repeating (Rhei).** Recurring practices tracked as daily choices, not rigid schedules. A short undo window keeps the engagement history honest.
- **Pomodoro with intent.** A 35-minute timer tied to a specific task, ending with an honest outcome (completed / fresh / postponed / force majeure / aborted).
- **Gardening.** A ritual for reviewing every active horizon, surfacing stale and never-gardened work distinctly.
